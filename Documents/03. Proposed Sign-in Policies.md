# 03. Proposed Sign-in Policies

When a sign-in falls under two policies, the stricter requirement overrides the looser one. An admin does not get staff MFA instead of a hardware key.

| Policy | Who it applies to | Condition | Control | Exceptions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Require MFA everywhere | Staff, clinic users and vendor guests | A person signs in to an app they already have permission to use | Authenticator app or hardware key. SMS does not count. HQ and the partner VPN do not skip this check. | Emergency accounts follow Policy 4. Shared clinic tablets follow the method in 04. Shared Tablet Sign-In. Automated services do not use this rule. |
| 2. Block older sign-in methods | User and service accounts | An account uses an older sign-in method that cannot support MFA, such as the IMAP sign-ins in this case | Block the sign-in. | Only the scanner and the old mail app. Each exception is limited to a named service account and the required application, approved for a defined period, and monitored until the older method is replaced. No exceptions for individual mailboxes. |
| 3. Protect administrator accounts | Admin accounts | An administrator signs in | Hardware key and a company-managed device. An authenticator app or SMS cannot replace the key. Block access if the device cannot be confirmed as compliant. | Emergency accounts follow Policy 4. |
| 4. Keep emergency access working | Both emergency accounts | An emergency account signs in | Hardware key, configured and tested independently of normal access controls, and an alert on every successful sign-in, including from the office. A company-managed device is not required. | None. Not for daily work. If the key cannot work during an outage, resolve that before enforcing the key requirement. Keep the alert on. |
| 5. Shorten sign-in sessions | Staff, clinic users and vendor guests | Entra evaluates access to a protected application after the 12-hour sign-in interval has elapsed | Require reauthentication. Set a 12-hour sign-in frequency in Entra. | Automated services are out of scope. Emergency accounts follow Policy 4. |
| 6. Shared clinic tablets | Front-desk staff using shared clinic tablets | Patient portal sign-in and tablet handover | Each staff member uses their own account and hardware key on a company-managed tablet. End the portal session before handover. The next employee signs in with their own account. | None. No shared accounts or IT-ticket bypasses. |
|
