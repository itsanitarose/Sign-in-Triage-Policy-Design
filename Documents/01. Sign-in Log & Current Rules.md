## Identity System Capabilities

Your identity system can: require MFA (authenticator app or hardware key), check whether a device is managed and compliant, allow or block by location, block older sign-in protocols, limit session length, and send an alert when a named account signs in.

<BR>

## Current Sign-in Rules

1. **MFA for all:** everyone needs MFA on every app, **except** when signing in from a "trusted location": the HQ office range (`203.0.113.0/24`) or the BillingBridge partner VPN (`198.51.100.0/24`). BillingBridge is a billing vendor whose staff use that VPN.
2. **Older protocols:** allowed. A scanner and an old mail app still use IMAP.
3. **Admins:** no separate rule. They follow rule 1.
4. **Vendor staff:** set up as guests. They follow rule 1.
5. **Emergency accounts:** two break-glass accounts exist for use if the identity system fails. They follow rule 1 and nobody is alerted when they sign in.
6. **Sessions:** people stay signed in for 90 days, on any device.
