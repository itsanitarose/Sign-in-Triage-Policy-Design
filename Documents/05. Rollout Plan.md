
# 05. Rollout Plan

Introduce the new rules in stages to avoid disrupting access.

- **Prepare.** Identify affected staff, admins, vendors, older systems and clinic tablets. Update older systems or approve temporary exceptions. Test emergency accounts, hardware keys and alerts. Confirm tablet compatibility.

- **Report-only.** Run the rules without enforcing them. Identify sign-ins that would fail and resolve problems first.

- **Pilot.** Test with IT, one vendor group and Clinic 12. Check sign-ins, tablet handovers and lost-key recovery.

- **Expand.** Apply the rules gradually. Tell affected staff and clinics what is changing and when. Avoid busy clinic hours for the tablet cutover. Monitor failed sign-ins, support requests and clinic access problems. Retire the shared account and remove IT-2201 only after Clinic 12 passes.

- **Rollback.** If essential access fails, reverse the affected rule, investigate and test again.
